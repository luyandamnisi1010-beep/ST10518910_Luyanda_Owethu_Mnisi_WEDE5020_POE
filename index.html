Oak & Steam is more than just a cafe.

Oak & Steam started in 2019 in Pretoria, South Africa. When two friends with a love for quality coffee, artisan tea and freshly baked goods decided to open a place where people can come and connect through what they're passionate about.

What started off small gradually grew into a friendly community space where people come to relax and put their minds at ease.
