Frequently Asked Questions

Does Oak & Steam support local suppliers? v

Yes. We aim to source fresh, locally produced ingredients whenever possible and support local suppliers in our community.

Does Oak & Steam sell halal food? v

All our meats are sourced from certified Halal suppliers, and our kitchen strictly avoids non-halal ingredients. Please ask our staff for detailed dietary information!

Are there accessible facilities available? v

Yes, our cafe is fully step-free with wide doorways and accessible restroom facilities to ensure a comfortable visit for all guests.
