About Us

Welcome to Oak & Steam, where every cup tells a story. Founded in 2019 by two friends who were passionate about fine coffee, handcrafted teas and freshly baked goods, the cafe has grown into a friendly place where people can unwind, work or catch up with friends. Our aim is to please every person that enters our doors with warm service, fresh products and a friendly atmosphere.

A Little Steam , A Lot Of Soul

In a welcoming and cozy set up Oak & Steam offers fine coffee, refreshing teas and baked favourites which a freshly baked for all kinds of moments. From a vey occuiped morning coffee to a relaxed afternoon with friends, our cafe offers something for everyone in a warm and comfortable setting. We focus on quality, Over the top service and supporting local suppliers whenever possible.
