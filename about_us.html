About Oak & Steam

Oak & Steam, Where High Quality Coffee , Artisan Tea and Freshly baked Goods are brewed with heart and shared with Everyone

A cafe built good tea, coffee and good connections.

Oak & Steam is a locally owned cafe that started in 2019. Founded by two friends. Oak & Steam was founded to be way more than just a place to grab coffee or tea, it was created to be your home away from home

Our Story

Oak & Steam began as a simple idea between the two friends: create a warm place where people can come together, enjoy quality food and drink while feeling at home.

In 2019, two friends turned their passion for coffee, tea and freshly baked goods into a small local cafe.

From that date, Oak & Steam has grown into a place that feels like home , a community space for students, families , local residents and visitors.

Some people visit us for coffee, others to study, work, catch up with friends or take a moment for themselves.

Through all, our goal has remained the same:
Create good moments around good food and drink.

Why Choose Us
